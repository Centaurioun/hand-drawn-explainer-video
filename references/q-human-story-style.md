# Natural-Skin-Tone Chibi Character Stories

Use for biographies, history, and real people's perspectives. Xiaohei suits abstract ideas and metaphors; do not turn real people into black mascot silhouettes.

## Character anchors

- Natural skin tones, complete human anatomy, approximately 3–4 heads tall; clear features and appealing outlines without excessive idealization.
- Record hairstyles, facial hair, face shapes, clothing, and representative props for young, middle-aged, and older stages.
- Keep face shape, glasses, clothing colors, and proportions consistent within each stage.
- Express events through gestures and props, not floating labels or long text.

## Composition rules

- White or warm-white paper, clear dark contours, limited flat colors, low texture, ample negative space.
- One event per scene; split multiple events into scenes unless they naturally form dual semantic islands.
- Do not generate logos, dates, product names, or long text in source images; overlay them deterministically.
- Reserve approximately the bottom 18% for subtitles; keep faces and key props outside it.

## Biography prompt addition

```text
Create a handsome, natural-skin chibi human portrait with a complete human body,
about 3.5 heads tall, in a clean hand-drawn editorial storybook style.
Preserve these age anchors: [hairstyle/facial hair/clothing/props].
Sparse dark outlines, limited flat color, low texture, generous negative space.
No black face or black body, no mascot replacement, no pseudo-text, no logo.
```

The Steve Jobs example uses long hair in youth, dark hair during the startup years, shorter hair in middle age, and gray facial hair and round glasses later. These are example-specific features, not a universal template.
