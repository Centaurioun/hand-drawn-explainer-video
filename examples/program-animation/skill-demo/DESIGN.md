# What Is a Skill? Complete Hand-Drawn Animation

## Style prompt
9:16 educational explainer. Warm-white paper, thick slightly irregular dark lines, blue-clothed office character, and yellow robot. Independent elements, hinge gestures, book pages, and process steps communicate ideas. Keep it clear and playful without imitating a creator's personal IP.

## Colors
- Paper #F8F6EF
- Ink #24251F
- Cobalt #2855C7
- Sunflower #F0C541
- Tomato #D64B36

## Typography
Arial/Helvetica for English text and Georgia for the Skill wordmark. Text is deterministic and separate from illustration.

## Motion
36-second timeline; eight scenes: repeated requests, organizing instructions, definition, weekly reports, supporting materials, reuse, permissions and checking, final definition. Paper crossfades; forearms and document components animate independently. Hold the final definition. The original included narration is Mandarin; English captions are translated, not newly aligned English speech.

## What NOT to Do
- No full-image pan/zoom as principal animation.
- No AI-generated text or new voice without authorization.
- No dark background, gradients, or drifting paper color.
- No decorative motion obscuring explanation.
- No claims of photorealistic or fully articulated animation.
